## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship (Week 2 Project.

The objective was to design an interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes Superstore's sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional performance, shipping efficiency, and discount impact between 2014 and 2017.

The dashboard was developed to support executive decision-making by transforming raw transactional data into actionable business insights and recommendations.

## Business Objectives

The analysis aimed to answer the following questions:
- How has sales and profit performance changed over time?
- Which regions, states, and cities generate the highest revenue and profit?
- Which customer segments contribute most to profitability?
- Which product categories and sub-categories perform best?
- How efficient are the company's shipping operations?
- What impact do discounts have on sales and profitability?
- What risks and growth opportunities exist within the business?

## Key Findings
- Sales increased from $484,247.47 in 2014 to $733,215.05 in 2017, representing a growth of 51.41%. Profit increased by 88.60% over the same period.
- The South Region showed stronger profitability than the Central Region despite generating lower sales.
- Texas and Philadelphia recorded significant losses, indicating areas requiring further investigation.
- Texas and Philadelphia recorded significant losses, indicating areas requiring further investigation.
- 793 customers generated approximately 5,000 orders.
- Furniture generated significant revenue but had the lowest profit margin (2.49%).
- Standard Class shipping generated 59.12% of total revenue. Standard shipping averages 5 days compared to an overall average delivery time of 4 days.
- Products with 0% discount generated the highest revenue and profit with a profit margin of 29.50%. Meanwhile, items with 50% or more discount had the lowest sales and generated a loss with a profit margin of -78.82%.

## Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-margin product categories.
- Review pricing and discount strategies within the Furniture category.
- Investigate drivers of losses in Texas and Philadelphia.
- Target Corporate and Home Office customers through tailored marketing initiatives.
- Improve Standard Class shipping efficiency.
- Monitor profitability trends regularly across products and regions.


### Limitations
- Analysis is limited to data from 2014–2017.
- Detailed cost data was unavailable.
- Customer satisfaction metrics were not provided.

### Assumptions  
- The provided data is accurate and complete.
- Sales and profit figures are correctly recorded.
- Repeat purchases indicate customer satisfaction.

## Conclusion
The Superstore business demonstrated strong growth between 2014 and 2017, achieving a 51.41% increase in sales and an 88.60% increase in profit. The company benefits from strong regional performance in the West, profitable Technology and Office Supplies categories, and loyal customers who make repeat purchases.

However, the analysis also revealed multiple opportunities for improvement, including optimizing discount strategies, improving Furniture profitability, reducing losses in underperforming markets, and enhancing shipping efficiency. By implementing the recommendations outlined in this report, Superstore can strengthen profitability, improve operational performance, and position itself for sustainable long-term growth.
